# LinkedIn Post — WhatsApp-First Announcement

**We're rethinking how home services work in Kenya.**

After months of building DumuWaks — a platform connecting homeowners with verified technicians for plumbing, electrical, carpentry, and more — my partner Marita and I sat down and asked a hard question:

Why are we asking people to download an app when they already have the one they need?

**WhatsApp.**

80%+ of Kenyan smartphone users are on it daily. Technicians already find work through scattered WhatsApp groups. Homeowners already ask for referrals there. The trust channel exists — it just has no structure, no verification, and no protection.

So we're going WhatsApp-first.

Here's what that means:

**For homeowners:**
- Book a verified technician by sending "Hi" on WhatsApp
- Select your service, describe the problem, share your location — all inside the chat
- Pay securely via M-Pesa, track your technician live, approve the work, rate and review
- Under 2 minutes, start to finish. No app download. No account creation.

**For technicians:**
- Receive job alerts on WhatsApp with one-tap Accept
- Update job status, get paid, view earnings — all from the same app you message your family in
- Onboard with just your phone: upload ID, set your skills, drop a pin for your service area

**For those without WhatsApp:**
- SMS booking via keyword commands (any phone)
- USSD for zero-internet, feature phone users
- Or simply call — our support team books on your behalf

**The web app stays** — for admin, analytics, technician portfolios, and corporate clients. But the front door is now the app already in everyone's pocket.

The infrastructure is built: AI-powered matching, M-Pesa escrow payments, real-time tracking, verified technician profiles, and a booking system that handles everything from request to review. What's changing is how people access it.

Kenya has 150,000+ skilled technicians. 1 in 5 Nairobi homes needs an emergency repair each year. The market is KES 45 billion annually — and it runs on phone calls, word of mouth, and guesswork.

We're building the structure it deserves. Starting with the channel it already trusts.

Rolling out in Nairobi. Scaling nationwide.

If you're in the home services, fintech, or last-mile logistics space in East Africa — I'd love to connect.
